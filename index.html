About the workshop

A gathering that keeps growing

Taiwan and Japan share mountains that rise steeply from warm seas, summers swept by typhoons, and biotas shaped by the same currents and flyways. We also share a generation of ecologists asking how organisms adapt, cooperate, and shift their ranges as the climate warms.

Welcome to a workshop that keeps on growing. As the long friendship between Taiwan and Japan continues to warm, this meeting — which we still fondly call a “workshop” — has grown with it, well beyond its original scale.

The program reflects that scale. Sessions span the breadth of ecology and evolution — including, but not limited to, behavioral ecology, population and community ecology, climate-change biology, macroecology, and evolutionary genomics. Groups wishing to go further are warmly encouraged to propose a symposium and open a deeper conversation between our two communities.

Call for symposium proposals Deadline 1 September 2026. To propose a symposium, please email shensf@sinica.edu.tw.

Scientific themes

What we will talk about

Six threads running through both countries' research, chosen because they invite direct comparison across the Taiwan-Japan gradient.

01

Behavioral & social evolution

Cooperation, breeding systems, and social structure, from insects to vertebrates, and the ecological conditions that favor them.

02

Evolutionary genomics

Genomic architecture of adaptation, including structural variants, supergenes, and the genetics of complex traits.

03

Macroecology & biogeography

Elevational and latitudinal gradients, range dynamics, and how diversity is distributed across two mountainous archipelagos.

04

Climate-change biology

Range shifts, thermal niches, and the conservation challenges of a warming climate for marine and terrestrial species alike.

05

Community & ecosystem ecology

Species interactions, coexistence, and the processes that assemble and maintain communities through time.

06

Theory & methods

Models, simulation, and computational approaches that connect mechanism to pattern across scales.

Program

Three days, at a glance

Plenary lectures open each of the first two days and close the last; concurrent sessions, symposia and posters fill the days between. Each contributed talk runs 15 minutes (a 12-minute talk plus 3 minutes for questions); with two parallel sessions, the program has room for up to 64 contributed talks, alongside the plenary lectures. Getting to Academia Sinica

B1C Auditorium, IBMS Building

The workshop is held at the B1C Auditorium, Institute of Biomedical Sciences (IBMS) Building, Academia Sinica（中央研究院）. Concurrent sessions and posters are held in the adjacent Interdisciplinary Research Building（跨領域研究大樓）.
128 Academia Road, Section 2, Nangang District, Taipei 115, Taiwan

  • By MRT: Take the Bannan Line (blue) to Nangang Exhibition Center, then bus or taxi (about 10 minutes) to Academia Sinica.
  • By bus: Routes 212, 270, and 小12 stop at the Academia Sinica gate.
  • From TPE / TSA airports: Roughly 45 minutes by taxi from Taoyuan, 25 minutes from Songshan.
